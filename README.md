# Drone phase, not TikTok phrase…
> **Lock in. Stay focused. Get it done.**

Lock-in is a lightweight focus tool designed to help you stay away from distracting apps and websites during a **gaming or focus phase**.
<sup>What a name, am I right?</sup>

## Blocked Apps

Lock-in currently supports blocking:

* YouTube
* TikTok
* Canva
* Roblox
* Claude
* OpenAI / ChatGPT
* Discord
* Figma

> More apps are coming soon.

## Bypass Protection

Lock-in is designed to make bypassing an active lock difficult.
On supported browsers, Lock-in can restrict access to browser extension management pages, helping prevent the blocker from simply being disabled.

**Current support:**

* ✅ Opera extension-management protection
* ⚠️ Other browsers may not have the same protection
* ❌ Blocking cannot normally be removed during an active gaming phase
* ✅ Recovery mechanism available if Lock-in breaks

> **Important:** Lock-in is currently in beta, so bypass protection may vary depending on your browser and system configuration.

## Gaming Phase

Start an automatic 30 minutes timer where your browser is USELESS.
